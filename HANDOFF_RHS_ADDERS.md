## 1. Project in one paragraph
Blake models Victoria in PLEXOS without WRL (Western Renewables Link) and VNI West, because those projects are at risk. This project closes when the adders are final, the equations are ready for that re-run, and the re-run's RHS has been checked. ## 4. How an adder suggestion is built (details in README "RHS offsets" and the two 2026-10-28 paragraphs)
- Fit: flat or Summer/NotSummer adder on month x half-hour-slot median gaps; tests = materiality, sign consistency (2/3 of months), leave-one-month-out gain. Fits under 9 months are `review`.
- Low-end fit: the same on the P10 of each cell. If the median fit gives nothing but P10 passes, the P10 adder is recommended (`fit_basis` = P10, `review`). A median adder well above the P10 gap is flagged "optimistic at low end".
- Uprating rule (Blake's): a positive ESOO RHS step does not end or reduce the adder; a downrating or missing data ends it. WRL-affected constraints ignore Grp6+ RHS changes in both scenarios.
- Cleaning: `SWAMP_CAP` drops MMS RHS above a cap (DDSM/MRDD 5,000 MW). `EXCLUDED` (SMTX2_SMTX1_R, LINC1_LITO1, DPTX3_DPTX4) get no adder and a reject.
- SIPS constraints (V>>V_NIL_DDSM1_DDSM2, V>>V_NIL_MRDD1_MRDB2): Nov-Mar is `action = set_rhs` to 10,000 MW until 2032-03-31 (neither binds in MMS during SIPS; ESOO binds them 0.2-0.5% of those hours; end date assumed from "until 2032"; confirm with Blake). Apr-Oct gets a fitted adder (DDSM +145, MRDD none). `rhs_offsets_to_apply.csv` has `action` (add_adder / set_rhs), `rhs_value_mw` and `months` columns.
- Diagnostics (information only): `lhs_bias_pct` (ESOO - MMS LHS as % of MMS RHS; flag over 15%); `headroom_gap_mw` (fit on RHS - LHS; over-binds taken at face value, never use it to set an adder); `util_adder_p95_mw` / `util_adder_p99_mw` (adder that matches the upper tail of LHS/RHS utilisation: the right way to balance LHS and RHS differences).

## 5. The review file `inputs\adder_review.csv`
Blake's columns: `decision` (accept / reject / override), `override_mw`, `comment`, `reviewed_ok`. Blank decision = the suggestion is applied as shown; `reject` drops the constraint's rows from `rhs_offsets_to_apply.csv`; `override` replaces adder MW only (not `set_rhs`). `y` in `reviewed_ok` means "I am OK with it" (confirmed by Blake). Script columns: `review_status` (NO CHANGE / NO CHANGE - NEW WARNING / RE-REVIEW / NEW / EXCLUDED), `what_changed`, suggestion columns, `util_matched_adder`, `previous_decision`, and bookkeeping `reviewed_suggestion` / `reviewed_reason` (never edit; they remember what Blake last reviewed so a flag persists until acted on). A flag clears when Blake enters a decision on a RE-REVIEW row or types `y`. Decisions made on a suggestion that later changed are moved to `previous_decision` and blanked. Never overwrite Blake's decisions or comments; suggestions change, decisions do not.
